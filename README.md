## Effect Of Remote Work On Mental Health

#### including software engineers, HR, designer, project manager, marketing and sales.

## Headline findings from the (EDA)

- Work location differences are modest: High-stress rates are 34.4% (Remote), 34.0% (Hybrid), 32.7% (Onsite). Mean social-isolation scores are essentially identical (2.96-3.01).
- Satisfaction gap: Remote workers report the lowest satisfaction with remote arrangements (30% Satisfied vs 36% Onsite).
- Mental-health conditions (Anxiety / Depression /Burnout / None) are evenly distributed; location does not uniquely elevate any condition.
- Hotspots: Finance & Healthcare industries; Data Scientist, Sales and Designer roles show slightly higher high-stress shares (~35%).
- Stronger signal: Sleep quality has a clearer directional relationship with stress than work location. Access to Mental Health resources alone is not associated with lower stress.

## Strategic Recommendations On Enhancing Remote Work Mental Health

1. Prioritise sleep & recovery.

2. Target high-stress roles and industries with tailored interventions.

3. Close the remote satisfaction gap (connection, tooling, manager capability).

4. Measure beyond the location label.

5. Audit the effectiveness of mental-health resources, not just access.
